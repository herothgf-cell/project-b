Vehicle interior recordings used in Eungye Taxi
All five source recordings were published under CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Engine: Car Acceleration Inside Car.wav by alexftw123
https://freesound.org/people/alexftw123/sounds/152661/
Road: Car Driving Interior by squareal
https://freesound.org/people/squareal/sounds/237374/
IndicatorOn and IndicatorOff: Car Indicator On Off.wav by wesleywestmusic
https://freesound.org/people/wesleywestmusic/sounds/644427/
Horn: Car Honk by qubodup
https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/185479/
Rain: Rain_insideCar by Brikaro
https://freesound.org/people/Brikaro/sounds/855678/

Adaptations use mono excerpts, filtering and level adjustment. Looping tracks
have crossfaded joins. Indicator clicks and the two horn taps are trimmed.
