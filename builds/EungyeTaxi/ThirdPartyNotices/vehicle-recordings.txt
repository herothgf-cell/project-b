All source recordings were published under CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

https://freesound.org/people/alexftw123/sounds/152661/
Road (highway cabin) and WindowWind: Highway drive interior by gecop
https://freesound.org/people/gecop/sounds/521766/

https://freesound.org/people/leonelmail/sounds/570461/
HardBrake: R04-12-Car Comes to a Stop.wav by craigsmith

https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/185479/
Rain: Rain_insideCar by Brikaro
https://freesound.org/people/Brikaro/sounds/855678/

Adaptations use mono excerpts, filtering and level adjustment. Looping tracks
have crossfaded joins. Indicator clicks and the two horn taps are trimmed.
On 2026-11-23 the Engine loop was level-smoothed (slow gain riding to remove a
periodic swell) and its loop join was re-crossfaded.
On 2026-11-24 the Road loop was replaced. Road, RoadSlow, WindowWind and
HardBrake were cut from the site's high-quality MP3 previews of the three
recordings above: 14 s excerpts, rumble filter, slow gain riding and 2 s loop
crossfades for the cabin loops; a mix of both cabin recordings with the low end
removed and the high end lifted for WindowWind; a 2 s squeal with the top end
rolled off for HardBrake. The earlier Road source (Car Driving Interior by
squareal) is no longer used.
